open source pcvr headset / orbsLAM3 / full asa

[deltavr.]

a pseudopancake pcvr headset with orbsLAM3 tracking running on the headset camera, processed by your pc. tmr controllers with hall triggers, everything printed in asa on a lightly-modified ender 3. built for stardance, now shipped.
